Immersive Works

Interactive experiences, VR escape rooms, and virtual spaces that blend narrative, atmosphere, and technology.

Interactive Experiences

Escape Room Horror Game (p5.js)

A web-based escape room built with p5.js. Players move through a series of scenes by clicking on interactive elements, solving puzzles that unlock the next stage. Custom graphics, ambient audio, and guided clues create a suspenseful but accessible experience focused on logic, observation, and gradual discovery.

Dancing Game (p5.js)

An interactive web dancing game where players respond to on-screen prompts in time with the music. Using p5.js for visuals and timing, the piece combines rhythm, color, and simple interactions to simulate a playful, arcade-like dance experience that trains timing and pattern recognition.

VR Escape Room: Fractured Identity (Unity)

This VR escape room places the player in an abandoned hospital with no clear memory of how they arrived. From a first-person view, they explore corridors and patient rooms, collecting records, keycards, notes, and tools that connect into a larger puzzle system. As clues are combined and passwords unlocked, new areas open and the tone shifts from quiet clinical to psychological tension. The core loop is simple: explore, collect clues, solve puzzles, unlock new spaces, and reach one of several endings. My role focused on interaction development, building object grabbing, inventory logic, and proximity-based triggers that turn the environment into a fully playable VR escape experience.

Virtual Worlds & Spaces

Cyberpunk VR World Inspired by XG "GRL GVNG"

This VR environment is inspired by XG's "GRL GVNG" and centers on a neon, cyberpunk city that combines futuristic infrastructure with themes of female empowerment and unity. Players move through elevated walkways, layered signage, and glowing streets that feel both rebellious and communal. The world design blends gang aesthetics with sisterhood, using color, scale, and motion to express strength, confidence, and solidarity. My work focused on worldbuilding, lighting, and spatial composition to translate the song's energy into an explorable 3D space.

Isolated Island Environment (Unreal Engine)

This environment study was built in Unreal Engine as a solitary island surrounded by open water. The focus of the project was atmosphere and mood rather than gameplay. I experimented with landscape sculpting, foliage placement, lighting, fog, and sky settings to create a sense of distance, isolation, and quiet tension. The piece functions as a research space for environmental storytelling and level composition, exploring how terrain, light, and horizon lines shape the emotional reading of a world.

WebXR Weapon Gallery

This WebXR project presents a small gallery of weapons that I modeled and textured myself. Viewers can access the experience directly in the browser, inspect each weapon in three dimensions, and move through the space as if walking through a digital display room. The project combines 3D modeling, material design, and WebXR interaction to explore how virtual objects can be staged, lit, and framed as artifacts. It also serves as a technical exercise in browser-based immersive media, connecting my VR work to web technologies and interactive spatial interfaces.
