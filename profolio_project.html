Hello!

I’m Jacqueline Wang, a digital content creator, game developer, and creative technologist studying Integrated Design & Media at NYU Tandon with a minor in Computer Science.

My work spans XR and VR world-building, game development, narrative design, and digital content creation. I have built projects including immersive VR environments, GameMaker and Unity prototypes, photography-based narratives, and self-directed performance videos.

My creative practice moves between digital storytelling and interactive systems. In XR projects such as Cyberpunk City and ASMR City, I designed 3D environments, built spatial interactions, and explored how atmosphere affects user emotion. In my game work, including laboratory-themed psychological horror prototypes and pixel-style action games, I developed mechanics, dialogue systems, and environmental narrative structures using Unity, GameMaker, and Blender.

I also direct and perform in visual projects, integrating choreography, cinematography, and digital media production to experiment with movement and narrative. Across these projects, I am interested in how technology shapes perception, how interactive spaces influence behavior, and how world-building can merge artistic intuition with computational design.
