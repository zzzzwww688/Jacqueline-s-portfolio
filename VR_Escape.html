VR Escape Room

Inspiration

I once played a small horror game where the protagonist unknowingly had a split personality. Each time the personality shifted, the camera perspective changed as well, leading the player to believe they were controlling two different characters. I found this narrative trick fascinating, and it inspired me to write a complete storyline of my own. I integrated this dual-identity concept into the VR escape room and built the puzzles, scenes, and progression around the gradual reveal of the truth.

Story

The experience places the player in a silent, deserted hospital with no memory of how they arrived. Through a first-person VR perspective, the player explores offices, corridors, and patient rooms, gradually uncovering fragments of identity hidden within medical files, keycards, and cryptic notes. Each item collected becomes part of a larger puzzle network that unlocks new spaces and reveals deeper narrative clues.

As the player pieces information together, the atmosphere shifts from clinical calm to psychological tension. Doubts about memory, perception, and reality begin to emerge, forcing the player to question what is true and what is imagined. The progression follows a core loop: explore, collect clues, solve puzzles, unlock new areas, and make a final choice.

The story branches into two endings depending on the player's decisions. One ending reveals the truth behind their identity and allows escape. The other keeps them trapped inside a fractured mental world, unable to confront reality.

Project Overview

This VR escape room places players inside a laboratory experiment gone wrong. Through environmental storytelling, digital props, hand interactions, and puzzle mechanics, players must uncover clues, navigate dangerous events, and finally escape the collapsing facility.

Objective: To explore immersive VR interaction, spatial storytelling, and atmospheric design.

Core Features

  • Full VR hand interaction (grabbing, rotating, pushing objects)
  • Timed danger mechanics that increase urgency
  • Environmental storytelling with layered clues
  • Immersive sound design & spatial audio

Tools & Methodology

  • Tools: Unity, XR Interaction Toolkit, Blender, Spatial Audio Design
  • Integrated VR locomotion, interaction events, puzzle logic scripting
  • Designed modular asset packs & optimized VR lighting

Outcomes

  • Successfully created a fully interactive VR environment
  • Implemented multi-step puzzle sequences and story-driven progression
  • Improved understanding of VR UX and player comfort

Future Improvements

  • Add more complex multi-room puzzles
  • Introduce AI-driven jump scare events
  • Optimize performance for standalone VR devices
